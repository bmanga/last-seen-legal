Last Seen Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Last Seen is a story game. We built it to keep your data on your phone.

  • No account. You don't sign up or log in, and we don't ask for your email address.
  • Your story stays on your device. Your name, pronouns, romance setting, choices and story progress are saved only on your phone.
  • Your profile photo stays on your device. If you choose a photo, the app opens your phone's photo picker, and the one photo you pick is copied into the app's own storage on your phone. It is never uploaded. The app can't see any of your other photos.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your phone so characters can text you while the game is closed. They are not sent from a server.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't use advertising or analytics tools, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share personal information.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Children. Last Seen is not intended for anyone under 16.
  • Deleting your data. Settings → Restart story erases your progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
