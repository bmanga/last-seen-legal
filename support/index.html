Last Seen Support

We usually reply within two business days: labs.cluno@gmail.com

  • Messages stopped arriving: check that notifications are allowed for Last Seen in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications. Characters don't message between 23:00 and 08:00 while quiet hours are on (in the game's Settings app).
  • Restore a purchase: open the store screen in the game and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Cancel or change your subscription: on your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Last Seen. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Free trial: you won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Start over: the game's Settings app → Restart story.
